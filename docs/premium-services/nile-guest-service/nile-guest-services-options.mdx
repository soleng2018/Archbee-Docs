Nile Guest Services offers the following authentication methods for securely onboarding guests onto the network:

- Click-Through
- Passcode
- Sponsored Guest Login (Email Approval)

**Click-Through**
Guests are granted network access after accepting the terms and conditions. Administrators can specify the allowed URLs that guests can access and also setup access for specific locations.



![](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/6swSwg1BT-BlfEPtYJhxM/Eb69bu6FO4i0WLeybKnEW_image.png)

**Passcode**
Guests must enter an :Comment[8-character alphanumeric]{docCommentId="eddnpubBEs0yD--_9YvrJ" resolved="false"} passcode to connect to the guest network. Administrators can set the passcode expiration as unlimited or specify start/end date ranges. Guest access can also be set up for specific locations.



![](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/6swSwg1BT-BlfEPtYJhxM/gxls7VprTUf6MXawC0nCs_image.png)



![](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/6swSwg1BT-BlfEPtYJhxM/cAlKX0as0Evoitkbge3A0_image.png "Captive Portal Page")

**Sponsored Guest Login (Email Approval)**
Guests provide their names and email addresses, and a sponsor must approve their access via email. Guests will be granted access once the terms and conditions have been accepted.
