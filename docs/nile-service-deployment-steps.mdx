## Step 2: Site Discovery, Cabling and Shipping

Each service begins with a complete, high-performance design based on your unique needs.

  <tr>
    <td>
      1&#x20;
    </td>
    <td>
      Site Discovery Scheduling
    </td>
    <td>
      Once the customer configures service areas in the Nile Portal, the Nile Service Delivery Manager (SDM) will contact the customer to schedule an on-site discovery for wired and wireless. A preferred partner of Nile will perform these site discovery activities
    </td>
    <td>
      Nile Staff
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      2
    </td>
    <td>

    </td>
    <td>
      After scheduling, Nile's preferred partner will perform the on-site data gathering and wireless site surveys, collecting essential information for the deployment plan
    </td>
    <td>
      Partner
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      3
    </td>
    <td>
      Deployment Plan
    </td>
    <td>
      Based on the survey findings, Nile will provide a detailed deployment plan for Access Points (APs), specifying placement and requirements
    </td>
    <td>
      Nile Staff
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      4
    </td>
    <td>
      Cabling
    </td>
    <td>
      After receiving the deployment plan, the customer is responsible for ensuring site readiness by arranging copper (CAT6A) cabling for Access Points and fiber (MMF/SMF) cabling between network closets to prepare for the installation phase
    </td>
    <td>
      Customer
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      5
    </td>
    <td>
      BoM and Shipping
    </td>
    <td>

    </td>
    <td>
      Nile Staff
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      6
    </td>
    <td>
      Installation Scheduling
    </td>
    <td>
      The Nile SDM will coordinate the installation schedule, working with the partner and the customer to confirm timing and finalize on-site installation plans
    </td>
    <td>
      SDM
    </td>
  </tr>
