## Access Switches

Nile has multiple Access Switches in its portfolio. These Access Switches are deployed based on the deployment requirements of the customer.&#x20;

The Nile **NSW 1000** switch is a Multigigabit Access Switch, supporting 100M to 5Gbps on all 48 ports, with built-in encryption on all uplink and access ports. The switch is highly scalable and provides data stacking, supporting a Ring bandwidth of 200Gbps. PoE is available on all 48 access ports, supporting power delivery up to 60W.

![NSW1000](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/x645AFhkNM_1_GWNpLhQC-3jFEaah2cL2yauPJmmo2L-20240806-184705.png "NSW1000")

The Nile **NSW 200** Switch is a high-performance Gigabit Ethernet switch supporting speeds of 10M to 1Gbps on all 48 ports. PoE is available on all 48 ports, with 8x ports supporting full 802.3bt type 4 power delivery up to 90W.

![NSW200](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/x645AFhkNM_1_GWNpLhQC-jTTY6XiTGoRVjhn4CXBm3-20240806-184811.png "NSW200")

The Nile **NSW 100** Switch is a high-performance Gigabit Ethernet switch supporting speeds of 10M to 1Gbps on all 24 ports. PoE is available on all 24 ports supporting full 802.3bt type 4 power delivery up to 90W.



The Nile **NSW 250&#x20;**&#x69;s a compact Switch that is Noise optimized and ideal for Branch office and small-medium Enterprise deployments. It is an enterprise-class multigigabit switch with 16 access ports that support 100M to 5Gbps speeds. NSW 250 supports ring bandwidth up to 200 Gbps. PoE is available on all 16 access ports, supporting power delivery up to 60W. The switch can be Wall mounted or Rack mounted.&#x20;

![NSW250](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/x645AFhkNM_1_GWNpLhQC-hEp882kguZ5QBZ8uitYi1-20240806-184900.png "NSW250")

## Distribution Switch

Nile Distribution/Aggregation switch provides an important layer of redundancy and scalability for the network, routes network traffic, and enforces network security policies. The Nile **NSW 1500** switch is an enterprise-class campus Distribution/Aggregation switch that supports multiple Access layer switches. It supports Multigigabit ports (10/25/40/100Gbps) with flexible uplink interfaces.&#x20;

## Access Point

Nile deploys Enterprise grade Access Points that align with our overall service goals of providing high-performance, secure, and managed wireless connectivity. The Nile Access Service is designed for quick and straightforward activation, ensuring a seamless Day 1 deployment experience. Using an intuitive mobile app and step-by-step guidance for installing and activating APs.

The **NWA 1000** is a WiFi6 Indoor Wireless Access Point with 3 radios and  includes Wireless features such as Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiple Access (OFDMA) and bidirectional multi-user MIMO for enhanced efficiency. The third radio acts as a monitoring sensor to continuously test the Nile Access Service’s availability, coverage, and capacity. It also does automatic channel planning, AP radios automatically orchestrate sophisticated auto-channel and power allocation for optimized performance in a given environment.&#x20;

## Nile Physical Sensors

Nile physical sensors **(NSE 1000)** are simple to install and operate, plugging directly into wall power outlets without requiring Ethernet or PoE. These sensors integrate seamlessly with Nile Access Points (APs) using a mobile app, running various tests to measure availability, coverage, and capacity, thereby mimicking real client devices. While physical sensors are recommended, Nile also offers virtual sensors for measurement purposes.
