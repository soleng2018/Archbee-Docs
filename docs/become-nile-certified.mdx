Nile certification shows you know how to set up, customize, and troubleshoot your Nile service. This page explains the training paths, what each certification requires, and how to get started.

***

## What You Need to Know

With Nile, you no longer manage network infrastructure. Nile runs the network as a service, and you decide how it's used. Nile certification shows you know how to set up, customize, and troubleshoot your side of that service.

**What it takes:** You watch the training videos, complete hands-on labs in a Nile-hosted lab environment, and pass a quiz at each level. Some labs contain deliberately broken configurations, so earning a certification shows you can troubleshoot, not just set up.

**Our recommendation:** If you deploy or manage Nile, earn **NCA before Day 1 handover** and **NCP within 30 days of go-live**.

**Not everyone needs full certification.** If you work on the Help Desk or in Day 2 Operations, you have a shorter, role-specific course. See [Training by Role](#training-by-role).

**To get your Nile Learning Portal account,** contact your Nile Sales representative or Service Delivery Manager (SDM), or email **[soleng@nile-global.com](mailto\:soleng@nile-global.com)**.

## How to Get Certified

1. **Request access.** Contact your Nile Sales representative or SDM, or email **[soleng@nile-global.com](mailto\:soleng@nile-global.com)**. Include your name, email address, company, and the certification you're working toward (NCA or NCP).
2. **Receive your Nile Learning Portal account.** Nile creates your account and enrolls you in Video Training, your courses, and your lab environment. The portal tracks your progress from here on.
3. **Watch the Video Training videos** on the Nile Learning Portal. See [Path 1](#path-1-video-training).
4. **Pass the Video Training quiz** with 80% or higher.
5. **Complete all 9 NCA labs** on the Nile Learning Portal.
6. **Pass the NCA quiz** with 80% or higher.
7. **Receive your NCA badge.**
8. **Continue to NCP.** Once you hold NCA, request NCP access the same way, complete all 12 NCP labs, pass the NCP quiz with 80% or higher, and receive your NCP badge.

All labs run in a Nile-hosted environment. You don't need your own Nile hardware.

You act on alerts for services your organization runs itself, such as your own DHCP or RADIUS servers.

## FAQ

**How long does each path take?**
Video Training: \[TIME\_TBD]. NCA: \[TIME\_TBD]. NCP: \[TIME\_TBD].

**Do I need my own Nile hardware?**
No. All labs run in a Nile-hosted lab environment.

**Do I need an account?**
Yes. You need a Nile Learning Portal account for the videos, labs, and quizzes, so your progress can be tracked. Nile creates it for you. Request one from your Nile Sales representative or SDM, or email [soleng@nile-global.com](mailto\:soleng@nile-global.com).

**Does everyone on my team need to be certified?**
No. IT Admins should earn NCA and NCP. Help Desk and Day 2 Operations staff take a shorter, role-specific course and earn a role badge. See [Training by Role](#training-by-role).

**Can I skip NCA and go straight to NCP?**
No. Video Training and NCA are both required before NCP.

**Can I just watch the videos without getting certified?**
Yes. Video Training doesn't lead to a certificate, and you can stop there. You still need a Nile Learning Portal account to watch the videos and take the quiz.

**How do I receive my certification?**
As a digital badge issued through the Nile Learning Portal.
