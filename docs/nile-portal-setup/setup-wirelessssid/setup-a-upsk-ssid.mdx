A Unique Passphrase (UPSK) SSID lets every user or device connect to the same Wi-Fi network using its own passphrase. If a passphrase is shared accidentally or a device is lost, you can revoke or change that passphrase without affecting other users or devices.&#x20;

By consolidating devices onto a single SSID, UPSK can reduce the number of PSK SSIDs and associated beacon and other management frames, helping reduce RF overhead and improve Wi-Fi airtime efficiency.

**Use UPSK for:**

- Devices that cannot authenticate with a username and password (802.1X), such as cameras, printers, TVs, and meeting-room equipment.
- Employee-owned devices, where employees authenticate through your identity provider and create a unique passphrase for each device.

## Prerequisites

Complete the following steps before creating a UPSK SSID, with SSO setup being optional. Each step has a dedicated page with detailed instructions.

- **Configure a Service Area.** Define the sites, buildings, and floors where you need Nile connectivity. See :Link[Setup Service Areas]{href="https://docs.nilesecure.com/setup-service-areas" newTab="true" hasDisabledNofollow="false"}.
- **Configure DHCP.** Choose Nile DHCP or an external DHCP server, and define the subnets your users will use. See :Link[Setup DHCP]{href="https://docs.nilesecure.com/setup-dhcp" newTab="true" hasDisabledNofollow="false"}.
- **Configure Segments.** Create a segment for this SSID and select a RADIUS server for each site in the segment. See :Link[Setup Segments]{href="https://docs.nilesecure.com/setup-segments" newTab="true" hasDisabledNofollow="false"}.
- **Configure SSO.** Integrate your identity provider (IdP) with Nile. For IdP integration, refer to Integrations > Identity Providers (IdP): Google Workspace, Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, or Azure Active Directory.

## Configuration Steps

1. Log in to the Nile Portal.
2. In the left navigation, select **Network Setup > Wireless**, click the '**+**' sign, and choose **Personal**.
3. **Name**: Enter the SSID name.
4. Select the **Hide SSID** checkbox to hide the SSID.
5. **Security**: Select **WPA2**.
   - **Note:** The **Unique passphrase** option is available only with WPA2. WPA3 is not available for a UPSK SSID.
6. Select the **Unique passphrase** checkbox to assign a unique passphrase per user or device for the SSID.
7. **Optional:** Select the **Enable SSO** checkbox to let employees sign in through your identity provider (IdP) and create passphrases for their own devices.
8. **Segments**: Select one or more segments/subnets for this SSID to separate device groups, such as cameras and printers. Each passphrase maps devices to its associated segment/subnet. For example, with Nile-UPSK, camera, printer, and meeting room passphrases can map to the Cameras, Printers, and Devices segments, respectively. All devices use the same SSID.
9. Click **Save** to complete configuration.

**Note:** UPSK networks operate only on the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands. They do not support WPA3 due to current security protocol limitations, and the 6 GHz band requires WPA3 security.

## Create Unique Passphrases

### Option 1: Create passphrases as an administrator

Use this option for headless devices (devices that cannot use 802.1X), such as cameras and printers.

1. Log in to the Nile Portal.
2. In the left navigation, select Network Setup > Access Management, and open the **Wireless** tab.
3. Click on the '**+**' icon and enter the following details:
   - **Select**: Choose **UPSK**.
   - **Name / User**: Enter a name for the device or person (for example, Lobby cameras).
   - **Tag**: Optional. Enter a label for your reference.
   - **OUI/MAC**: Optional. Enter the MAC address of the device.
   - **SSID**: Select the UPSK SSID that the passphrase will work on.
   - **Segments**: Select the segment the device joins when it uses this passphrase. Choose one of the segments selected on the SSID.
   - **UPSK**: Click the refresh icon to have Nile generate a passphrase, or enter one manually. Passphrases follow the same rules as a PSK SSID and must be between 8 and 64 characters long.
4. Click **Save** to complete configuration.
5. Share the passphrase with the user, or enter it on the device.

**Notes:**&#x20;

- If you enter a MAC address, turn off the private (random) Wi-Fi address on that device for this network first. Otherwise, the device may connect with a different address from the one you entered.
- CSV upload is not currently supported for UPSK passphrase creation.

### Option 2: Employee self-registration

Use this option for employee-owned devices such as laptops, phones, and tablets. It requires the **Enable SSO** checkbox on the SSID and an SSO integration (see Setup SSO).

1. When SSO and a UPSK SSID are enabled, Nile generates a self-registration link.
2. Share the link with your employees. The link is available in the following places:
   - The my.nilesecure.com page, for users connected to the Nile network.
   - The Nile-hosted guest page.
   - The Nile Portal, where you can copy it and share it from anywhere.
3. Employees open the link and sign in through your identity provider.
4. Employees create a passphrase for their device and connect to the SSID with it.

**Note:** Wireless devices that employees register join the segment selected on the SSID. By default, each user can have up to 3 passphrases and up to 5 devices. These limits can be changed for your organization.
