## Nile Staff On-boarding

Welcome to **Nile Academy** — your professional learning hub powered by **Skillsoft Percipio**. This guide provides comprehensive instructions for requesting access and logging in for the first time.

**Step 1: Request Access via IT Support**

1. Navigate to the **Nile IT Support Portal**.
2. Create a new **IT ticket** with the following details:
   - **Category:** Access / Application
   - **Application Name:** Nile Academy (Percipio)
   - **Description:** Request for access to Nile Academy for training and development purposes.
3. Upon approval of your request, the **IT Team** will deploy the **Percipio app** to your **Okta dashboard**.
4. You will receive a communication from the IT team once the app has been assigned.

### Step 2: Access Nile Academy via SSO

Once you have the access, you can see the app on your **Okta Dashboard&#x20;**&#x61;nd you can log in to Nile Academy using **Single Sign-On (SSO)**.&#x20;

<Paragraph indent="1">
  Option 1 — Direct URL: Visit [https://nile.percipio.com](https://nile.percipio.com)
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph indent="1">
  Option 2 — Through Okta: [https://nile-global.okta.com/](https://nile-global.okta.com/)
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" indent="2">
  Access your Okta Dashboard.
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" listStart="2" indent="2">
  Find & select the **Percipio** (Nile Academy) application tile.
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" listStart="3" indent="2">
  You will be redirected to the Nile Academy login page.
</Paragraph>

### Step 3: Login Process

1. **Login Page**

<Paragraph indent="1">
  You will be presented with the Nile Academy login screen. Click *“Login with your corporate credentials.” for SSO Login.*
</Paragraph>

2. **Okta Authentication**

<Paragraph indent="1">
  You will be directed to the **Okta login page**. Input your **corporate email address** and click **Next**.
</Paragraph>

3. **Password Verification**

<Paragraph indent="1">
  Enter your **Okta password** and click **Verify**.
</Paragraph>

4. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**

<Paragraph indent="1">
  Choose your preferred verification method:
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" indent="2">
  Email
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" listStart="2" indent="2">
  Google Authenticator
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" listStart="3" indent="2">
  Security Key / Biometric
</Paragraph>

### Step 4: Access Confirmed

<Paragraph indent="1">
  Upon successful login, you will be redirected to the **Nile Academy Dashboard** on Percipio.
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph indent="1">
  You may now:
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" indent="2">
  Explore available courses and certifications
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" listStart="2" indent="2">
  Monitor your learning progress
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" listStart="3" indent="2">
  Download completion certificates
</Paragraph>

### Need Assistance?

If you encounter any access-related issues:

- Please contact **[ITSupport@nilesecure.com](mailto\:ITSupport@nilesecure.com)** for assistance with access issues.
- For inquiries related to learning content and badging, reach out to [badging@nilesecure.com](mailto\:badging@nilesecure.com).
