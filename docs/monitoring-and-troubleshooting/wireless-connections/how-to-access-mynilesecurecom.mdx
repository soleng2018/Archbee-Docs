To access :Link[my.nilesecure.com]{href="https://my.nilesecure.com" newTab="true" hasDisabledNofollow="false"}, please follow the below steps:

1. Connect to any Wireless SSID within the Nile environment.
2. From your PC/Laptop/Mobile, open a browser and type "*my.nilesecure.com* " and click enter.
3. You will be able to see various information like the below screenshot.

![](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/6swSwg1BT-BlfEPtYJhxM/t83Q-ucuQb0de5ynGvVmZ_zovwxzd-cxycej4ode9ogj1agqwtnwylpq.png)

**This page will have the following information:**

1. Video conferencing signal quality of your PC/Laptop/Mobile.
2. Wireless Signal strength
3. Reachability from your device to the internet.
4. Option to test your device Speed.
5. Device location.
6. Application level reach

There is an **advanced option** available as seen below:

![](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/6swSwg1BT-BlfEPtYJhxM/9ZEWTlJfEt6m1-JqNhFSg_gl76w-hnprufkrnaxwrqpzmv1-r-mwmwoa.png)

Whenever you face any wireless issues on your end device, please attach the above information with the advanced option enabled from your device as a screenshot on the ticket.
