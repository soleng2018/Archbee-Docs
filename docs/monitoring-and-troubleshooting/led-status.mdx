<table isTableHeaderOn="true" columnWidths="181">
  <tr>
    <td>
      ### Sys LED Status
    </td>
    <td>
      ### Condition
    </td>
    <td>
      ### Recommended Action
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#09ff31">Blinking Green</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      System Initializing
    </td>
    <td>
      It is a transient state during the initial bring-up of the switch; if the LED stays persistent, contact Nile Support.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#00ff0a">Solid Green</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      NE is connected to both the NSB GW and the Nile Cloud, and the Nile Access Service is operational.
    </td>
    <td>
      \- No action is necessary. The Nile Access Service is operational and connected to Cloud.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#0011ff">Blinking Blue</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      Bluetooth enabled and available. Connection to the Nile Cloud and the local NSB gateway is not established, and BLE is ready for pairing.
    </td>
    <td>
      \- If it is new/out of the box, proceed with the setup.&#x20;
      \- If still in the same state after activation:&#x20;
      &#x20;  \- Verify connectivity issues between the NE and the Nile Cloud (e.g., firewall, routing).&#x20;
      &#x20;  \- Check for cable issues between the NE and NSB Gateway.&#x20;
      &#x20;  \- Investigate if the network closet is isolated from the rest of the network in case of multiple affected NEs.&#x20;
      &#x20;  \- Ensure the NE is activated through the mobile app.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#0011ff">Solid Blue</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      Bluetooth connected to the mobile app
    </td>
    <td>
      \- If the mobile app hasn’t paired to the NE for troubleshooting, but the LED is solid blue, reboot the switch.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#ff0000">Blinking Red</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      NE has connected either to the NSB GW or the Nile Cloud.
    </td>
    <td>
      \- The NEs might have lost connectivity to the cloud. Check firewall rules and ISP issues.&#x20;
      \- If the NSB GW is down, Nile will proactively notify you, and a Nile CSM will reach out.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#ff0000">Solid Red</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      Most probably a system failure if the LED is solid red for more than 20-30 seconds.
    </td>
    <td>
      \- If the persistent state is solid red, contact Nile Support.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>



<table isTableHeaderOn="true">
  <tr>
    <td>
      ### Power LED Status
    </td>
    <td>
      ### Condition
    </td>
    <td>
      ### Recommended Action
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#1dff00">Solid Green</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      The power module is okay and delivering the correct power.
    </td>
    <td>
      \- No action necessary
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#ff0000">Blinking Red</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      The power input is okay, but the PSU output failed, or the power module is not inserted properly.
    </td>
    <td>
      \- Reseat the power module/cable.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <font color="#ff0303">Solid Red</font>
    </td>
    <td>
      Either one of the two power cords is connected, or one of the power supply modules failed.
    </td>
    <td>
      \- Verify both power supplies are connected and providing power.&#x20;
      \- Check if the UPS is working fine.
      \- Check the PSU LED at the back of the switch:&#x20;
      &#x20;  \- Amber indicates active but not receiving power.&#x20;
      &#x20;  \- No LED indicates a PSU issue.&#x20;
      &#x20;  \- Contact Nile Support if needed.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
