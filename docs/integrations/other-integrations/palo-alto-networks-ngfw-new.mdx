### Overview

:Comment[The Nile Service Block (NSB) connects to one or two upstream Palo Alto Next Generation Firewall (NGFW) appliances, over point-to-point layer 3 links, and pushes all client traffic (north-south or east west) to the NGFW appliance. Such design puts the customer in full control of the NSB traffic, to implement the desired security policies.]{docCommentId="WBDUxENQDMHrtsA3LmRsU" resolved="true"}

The purpose of this document is to assist with the seamless integration between the Nile NSB and the Palo Alto NGFW appliance.

### Requirements

- Administrator rights to the Nile Portal.
- **NSB IP pool**:
  This is the IP pool used for the management plane of the NSB Elements to communicate with the Nile Cloud.
- **Sensor IP pool**:
  This is the IP pool used for the management plane of the Nile Sensors to communicate with the Nile Cloud.
- **Four /30 subnets**:
  These are the four (4) Equal Cost Multi-Path (ECMP) L3 links between the Nile gateways and the Palo Alto NGFW appliance, to achieve a High-Definition and Always-On service.
- **Client subnets**:
  These are the subnets defined on the Nile Portal and used by wired and wireless devices connected to the Nile Service.
- **Servers Addresses**:
  These are the IP addresses of the customer DHCP, DNS, and RADIUS servers that are to be defined on the Nile Portal.

### 4. Firewall Rules

By default, Palo Alto Next Gen firewall allows intrazone (within the same zone) traffic and denies / blocks interzone (between two different zones) traffic.

As three zones were created in this document – (1) WAN, (2) Nile and (3) DC – rules are needed to allow traffic from the Nile zone to the WAN zone, and between the Nile and DC zones.


Figure 27

![](https://api.archbee.com/api/optimize/6swSwg1BT-BlfEPtYJhxM/MFQOI-anRAi0IAVNsStJL_screenshot-2026-03-30-at-21831apm.png)

**4.1 Nile Access to the Internet**

Go to **Policies > Security**
Click on **+ Add** to create the policy as follows:

- **Name:** Nile to Internet
- **Rule Type:** Universal
- **Source** column set:
  - **Source Zone:** Nile
  - **Source Address:** Any
  - **Source User:** Any
  - **Source HIP Profile** Any
- **Destination** column set:
  - **Destination Zone:** WAN
  - **Destination Address:** Any
- **Application:** Any
- **Service/URL Category:** Any
- **Action:** Allow

**4.2 Nile DC Access**

Go to **Policy > Security**
Click on **+ Add** to create the policy as follows:

- **Name:** Nile DC traffic
- **Rule Type:** Universal
- **Source:**
  - **Source Zone:** DC and Nile
  - **Source Address:** Any
  - **Source User:** Any
  - **Source HIP Profile** Any
- **Destination:**
  - **Destination Zone:** DC and Nile
  - **Destination Address:** Any
- **Application:** Any
- **Service/URL Category:** Any
- **Action:** Allow

NOTE

**The above rules are provided as an example; it is up to customers to change them according to the requirements of their security policies.**
