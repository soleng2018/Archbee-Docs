# Introduction

Nile Service Alerts raise awareness about Nile Service's performance guarantees - availability, capacity and coverage.

Below is the list of alerts raised in the Nile Portal for Nile Service performance guarantees.

***

## Nile Service Capacity Guarantee Violation

This alert is generated when Nile service is operating at less than guaranteed capacity.

<table isTableHeaderOn="true">
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Example Alert
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      Nile service is operating at less than provisioned capacity at Bangalore/Bldg\_1/Floor\_1 for 5 minutes
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Recommended Customer Action
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      • Check for alerts: Nile access point down
      • Check for alerts: sensor down
      • Check for alerts: link down
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

***

## Nile Service Coverage Guarantee Violation

This alert is generated when Nile service is operating at less than guaranteed WiFi coverage.

  <tr>

  </tr>

    </td>

</table>
