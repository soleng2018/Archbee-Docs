# Introduction

Nile infrastructure alerts raise awareness about the issues experienced by Nile-owned services such as Nile Service Block (NSB), Nile Guest Service, and Nile DHCP service.

Below is the list of alerts raised in the Nile Portal for Nile Infrastructure, along with recommended actions.

***

## Device Unavailable

This alert is generated when a Nile service element, such as switches, access points, or sensors go offline. The user can view the details on offline network elements by clicking the alert and visiting the "Nile Device Inventory" page.&#x20;

<table isTableHeaderOn="true" columnWidths="330,331">
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Example Alert
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      Nile Service Element switch AS1 Serial Number: E00A00064619 has lost power at Bangalore/Bldg\_1/ Floor\_1. for 1 day 6 hours 6 minutes
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Recommended Customer Action
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      • Ensure that the device is powered on and ready.
      • Check for unplugged or damaged cables.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## Renegotiation to Lower Speed - Nile Service Block Links

This alert is generated when a network link is operating at a lower speed than its provisioned capacity. The alert covers both intra-NSB links (Access Point to Switch, Switch to Switch) and NSB to Internet links (DS-ISP). These conditions are typically caused by devices renegotiating to a lower speed, often due to a faulty cable.&#x20;



<table isTableHeaderOn="true" columnWidths="330,331">

      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        **Switch to Switch Link**
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        Link between Nile switch E00A00064610 port 12 and Nile switch E00A00064619 port 11 is operating at a lower than the provisioned capacity at  Bangalore/Bldg\_1/Floor\_1 for 40 minutes.
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        **NSB to Internet Link**
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        Link between Nile switch E00A00064610 port 12 and ISP router port 11 is operating at a lower than the provisioned capacity at  Bangalore/Bldg\_1/Floor\_1 for 40 minutes.

## Flapping Links&#x20;

This alert is generated when a network link flaps continuously.  The alert covers both intra-NSB links (Access Point to Switch, Switch to Switch) and NSB to Internet links (DS-ISP). These conditions are typically caused by a faulty cable.&#x20;



<table isTableHeaderOn="true" columnWidths="330,331">
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Example Alerts&#x20;
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      **Access Point to Switch Link:**
      <Paragraph>
        Link between port 11 of Nile switch E00A00064619 and Nile access point AP 1000 and is flapping freuently at  Bangalore for 40 minutes.
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        **Switch to Switch Link**
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        Link between port 12 of Nile switch E00A00064610 and port 11 of Nile switch E00A00064619 is flapping frequently at  Bangalore for 40 minutes.
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        **Switch to HeadEnd Link**
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        Link between port 12 of Nile switch E00A00064610 and port 11 of Headend HE00A00064619  is flapping frequently at  Bangalore for 40 minutes.
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        **Switch to Internet Link**
      </Paragraph>
      <Paragraph>
        Link between port 12 of Nile switch E00A00064610 and port 11 of ISP router  is flapping frequently at  Bangalore minutes.
      </Paragraph>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Recommended Customer Action
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      **Switch to Switch or Switch to Headend or Switch to ISP Router Link:**
      - Check for sharp bends along the fiber length
      - Reseat the SFP
      - Clean the fiber heads and reinsert the fiber into the SFP
      <Paragraph>
        **Switch to Access Point Link**
      </Paragraph>
      - Verify the cabling. Check patch panels and consider replacing the cabling
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## NSB to Internet Link Saturation

Nile portal generates an alert when the NSB to Internet link is operating close to its maximum capacity



<table isTableHeaderOn="true" columnWidths="330,331">
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Example Alerts&#x20;
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      Link between uplink port 0/35 of Nile switch E998567 and port 0/36 on Switch XYZ at BLR site is operating close to its capacity for 15 minutes. Maximum link bandwidth is 1 Gbps and utilized link bandwidth is 900Mbps&#x20;
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="left">
      Recommended Customer Action
    </td>
    <td align="left">
      - Audit any change in capacity needs
      - Consider moving to a higher bandwidth link between the Nile Service Block and uplink router/firewall
      - If the ISP link is saturating as well, consider upgrading to a higher bandwidth plan
      - Please reach out to support for assistance with upgrading the link between the Nile service block and uplink router/firewall
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
